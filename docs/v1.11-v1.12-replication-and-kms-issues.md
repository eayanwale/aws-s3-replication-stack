# v1.11–v1.12 — Cross-Region + Cross-Account Replication (and the KMS Issues)

**Branches:** `STC15-1430_Enable_Cross_Region_Replication_v1.11`, `STC15-1431_Enable_Cross_Account_Replication_v1.12`

---

## v1.11 — Cross-Region Replication (STC15-1430)

Added `aws_s3_bucket_replication_configuration` to replicate objects to a bucket in a different AWS region.

**Why:** Cross-region replication provides geographic redundancy — if an AWS region has an outage, objects are still available in the replica bucket. It also enables lower-latency reads for users in the replica region.

**Prerequisites met:**
- Versioning enabled (v1.5) — required; replication only works on versioned buckets
- KMS encryption configured (v1.7) — the replication IAM role needs explicit permission to use the KMS key

---

## v1.12 — Cross-Account Replication (STC15-1431)

Extended replication to a bucket owned by a different AWS account.

**Why:** Cross-account replication enables disaster recovery to a separate, isolated account. Even if the source account is compromised or accidentally deletes the bucket, the replica in the separate account is unaffected.

**Additional complexity vs. cross-region:**
- The destination bucket's account must add a bucket policy granting the source account's replication role `s3:ReplicateObject` and `s3:ReplicateDelete`
- The destination account must grant KMS key permissions if the destination bucket is also KMS-encrypted
- The replication role in the source account must be trusted by the destination account

## Issues: KMS Login Failures + Variable Inconsistencies (post-merge fixes)

After merging v1.12, replication was failing silently. Objects were not appearing in the destination bucket.

### Issue 1: KMS Grants Missing (commit `93a4ac0`)

The replication IAM role had permission to read objects from the source bucket but didn't have permission to **use the source KMS key** to decrypt them before replicating. S3 replication decrypts objects to read them and re-encrypts at the destination.

**Fix:** Added the required KMS grants to `encryption.tf` and restructured `replica.tf` to clearly separate cross-region and cross-account replication configurations with their respective IAM policies.

The key additions:
- `kms:Decrypt` for the source bucket's KMS key (replication role needs to decrypt source objects)
- `kms:GenerateDataKey` for the destination bucket's KMS key (replication role needs to encrypt at destination)
- Explicit resource ARN scoping on both grants

### Issue 2: Variable/Resource Naming Inconsistencies (commit `8a06761`)

After the KMS fix, `terraform plan` was still failing with "variable not found" and "resource not found" errors. The refactor in `93a4ac0` introduced naming mismatches between what `replica.tf` referenced and what `encryption.tf` and `vars.tf` exported.

**Fix:** Resolved variable name mismatches, aligned resource references across files, and separated region replication config from account replication config into `replica_region.tf` and `replica_account.tf` for clarity.

---

## Lesson

Cross-account replication with KMS encryption requires grants at three levels:
1. **IAM policy** on the replication role — allows S3 actions
2. **KMS key policy** on the source key — allows the replication role to `kms:Decrypt`
3. **KMS key policy** on the destination key — allows the replication role to `kms:GenerateDataKey`

Missing any one of these silently fails replication — S3 doesn't always surface KMS permission errors as explicit failures in the console.
